Happy Birthday Papa ❤️

The girl who brought light into my quiet world…
You were the first, the unexpected, and the unforgettable.
Today’s not just your birthday — it’s a reminder of how lucky I am to have known you.

This was our first photo together — the first day we met.
I can never forget that day. Maybe we weren’t very close yet, but I still had a truly memorable time.

You sat beside me while watching the movie — and honestly, I didn’t even care about the film.
I was just staring at you the whole time, lost in that moment.

We met again after a little gap. I had so much to say…
But all I could do was look at you. You said it was awkward — I thought it was perfect.
We shared time, smiles, and a silence that meant everything.

That moment, I felt distance creeping in… and it scared me.
But then you messaged me — I wasn’t expecting it.
April became *our* month. The closeness, the comfort — it all grew deeper.

Just when everything felt so right, life reminded us of our priorities.
Two years of silence… not as strangers, but as two people choosing sacrifice over comfort.
For dreams. For careers. For the future we both deserve.

I miss you, Papa.
